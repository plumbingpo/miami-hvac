Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. How Cape Coral Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
